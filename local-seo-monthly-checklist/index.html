How much does local SEO cost? Judge it by what gets done each month

By Rocky V, Head of Operations at DigitalCave · 27 November 2026

The short answer: local SEO is priced by the amount of work, and the work should be visible. Before you compare monthly fees, ask each provider for a list of what they will do every month and what you will see at the end of it. A cheap plan with no visible work costs more than a dearer one that moves your map ranking and enquiries.

"How much does local SEO cost?" is one of the most common questions small businesses search. The honest answer depends on how competitive your area is and how much needs fixing. What you can control is whether you are paying for real work. Use this checklist to find out.

What a monthly local SEO plan should include

  1. Google Business Profile work. Regular posts, new photos, answered questions, checked categories and services. Google's own help on adding and managing your Business Profile shows what owners can do; a provider should be doing more than you would.
  2. Review management. A simple way to ask happy customers for reviews, and replies to every review, good or bad.
  3. Service and location pages. Improving or adding pages for the services and towns you want to be found for, with real detail rather than copied text.
  4. Citations and consistency. Checking your name, address and phone number match across the main directories, and fixing the ones that do not.
  5. Technical checks. Speed, mobile layout and errors reported in Search Console, fixed or passed to whoever looks after the site.
  6. A report that mentions enquiries. Calls, direction requests, form fills and where you rank in the map results for your main searches, compared with last month.

How to check the work yourself

  • Open your Business Profile and look at the date of the last post and photo.
  • Count your reviews this month and check every one has a reply.
  • Ask which pages on your site were changed this month, and look at them.
  • Search your main service plus your town once a month in a private window and note your map position.

Warning signs in a local SEO quote

  • Guaranteed first place in the map results.
  • Hundreds of directory listings for a one-off fee.
  • No access for you to your own profile or Search Console.
  • Reports that only show traffic and never mention calls or enquiries.
