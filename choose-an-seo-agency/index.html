How to choose an SEO agency: 10 questions to ask before you sign

By Rocky V, Head of Operations at DigitalCave · 24 November 2026

The short answer: a good SEO agency can tell you, in plain terms, what it will do in the first 90 days, how it will measure progress in enquiries rather than rankings alone, and what happens if you want to leave. If an agency cannot answer those three things clearly, keep looking.

Choosing an SEO agency is hard because most of the work is invisible and results take months. These ten questions, asked of every agency on your shortlist, make the differences much easier to see.

The ten questions

  1. What will you do in the first 90 days? Expect a concrete list: a technical check, fixing what blocks Google, improving key service pages, setting up tracking. Vague promises of "optimisation" are a warning sign.
  2. Which searches will you target first, and why? A good agency picks phrases your customers actually use, with a realistic chance of ranking, not just the biggest numbers.
  3. How will you report progress? Ask to see a sample report. It should connect rankings and traffic to calls, forms and sales.
  4. Who will do the work? Find out who your contact is and whether the work is done in-house or passed on.
  5. What do you need from us? SEO goes faster when you supply photos, case studies and answers to customer questions. A good agency tells you this up front.
  6. How do you build links and mentions? Listen for local partnerships, useful content and genuine directories. Bought links and private networks put your site at risk.
  7. Will we own everything? Your website, Google Business Profile, Search Console and analytics accounts should be in your name, with the agency added as a user.
  8. What is the contract length and notice period? Know how you can leave before you sign.
  9. Can we speak to a current client? A short call with someone in a similar business tells you more than any case study.
  10. What results can you not promise? Honest agencies will say they cannot guarantee a number one position. Anyone who does is guessing.

Red flags to walk away from

  • Guaranteed rankings or "page one in 30 days" promises.
  • Reports full of numbers that never mention enquiries.
  • Refusing to give you access to your own accounts.
  • Large link packages with no explanation of where the links come from.

Compare like for like

Send every agency the same short brief and ask for answers in writing. Put the answers side by side and choose the one that explains things most clearly, not the one with the most confident sales pitch.
